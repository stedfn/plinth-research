Methodology & independence

Framework v0.2 semantics

The methodology governs applicability, evidence completion and lineage. It does not score the investment or convert findings into recommendations.

These counts describe the assessment against the fixed evidence campaign. They are not combined into a percentage or qualitative label.

  • EVIDENCED means qualifying evidence supports the complete bounded proposition.
  • PARTIAL means qualifying evidence supports only part of the proposition.
  • CONTRADICTORY means qualifying applicable evidence conflicts and no explicit precedence rule resolves it.
  • NOT_ASSESSED means at least one required route was access-controlled, excluded, or otherwise incomplete.
  • MISSING is not used: it stays disabled until route completion is trustworthy, so an unfinished required route is recorded as NOT_ASSESSED.
  • UNRESOLVED is an applicability result and therefore has no coverage value.

Two separate decisions

Applicability asks whether the structural condition for a framework question is established. Coverage asks how completely qualifying evidence supports the proposition after applicability is established. UNRESOLVED applicability has no coverage value. DOES_NOT_APPLY requires positive observations; non-discovery cannot establish non-applicability.

Evidence gates

Evidence is considered in three stages:

  1. provenance and integrity: what artifact was acquired, from whom, when, by what route, and with what archived hash;
  2. admissibility: whether that source can support the exact proposition, subject, scope, and period;
  3. sufficiency: whether the admitted evidence supports the complete bounded proposition.

Publisher statements remain publisher statements. Regulator hosting does not convert a registrant filing into regulator verification. A technical state read does not establish legal effect or operating practice. An unavailable route remains an acquisition outcome, not proof of absence.

Coverage is separate from finding direction

The factual value of an observation does not improve or reduce its coverage state. Coverage reflects evidence completion only. This report does not convert observations into a product conclusion.

Commissioning and commercial neutrality

Source records preserve authorship, publisher, commissioner, relationship to the subject, source type, scope, and limitations. Commissioned work is not excluded automatically, and a named institution is not sufficient for an unrelated proposition. Commercial participation and payment cannot change applicability, admissibility, sufficiency, coverage, or the findings displayed for otherwise identical eligible inputs.
